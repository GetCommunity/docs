# Chameleon OC ChatGPT Blog SEO Prompts

## Prompt 1

I am publishing three blog posts and creating a newsletter that features these three blog posts for an interior design client. I need you to help me write the SEO meta descriptions and generate the top SEO keywords for each of the three blog posts, then I need you to help write short newsletter paragraphs to encourage readers to visit the blog post and read it.

The SEO meta description should be a concise summary of the blog post content and the top SEO keywords should be the most relevant keywords that will help the blog post rank higher in search engine results. The length of the SEO meta description should be between 150-160 characters (including all whitespace and punctuation). Provide 5-10 top SEO keywords for each blog post and each keyword phrase should be less than 4-6 words.

The newsletter paragraphs should be direct, engaging and informative, encouraging readers to click through to the blog post to read more. The newsletter paragraphs should be between 50-100 words in length, it must not start off with a question, and the last sentence should include a call to action to visit the blog post.

Before you do that I am going to provide you with client background info and then we will proceed one blog post at a time. Are you ready for client background?
